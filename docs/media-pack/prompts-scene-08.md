BOTH doors are fully open, believable metal locking bars and hinges visible; the dry pale interior, floor and depth remain readable. Modest scuffs, structurally sound. No invented branding or readable serials.

A normal yellow manual pallet truck has its forks correctly inserted under that pallet. Natural body proportions and a credible physical grip; avoid poses suggesting a floating load. A smaller matching crate is already further inside, leaving a clear path.
Critical geometry: the dock surface and container floor are at the SAME height, joined by a short flat dock plate. No step, steep ramp or floating pallet. Camera stands on the loading dock looking into the container, three-quarter view, around eye height; all of the main doorway, both doors, hero crate and worker fit with comfortable margins. The container is full-size and the wooden crate is plausible pallet scale, not a toy-sized container.
Surroundings: a glimpse of the same pale stone and grey-glass research-centre service facade and autumn foliage at the outer edge. Soft natural daylight, same sober documentary colour treatment as previous scenes. The person is a fictional logistics worker, not a redesigned member of the approved visitor trio.
Keep detailed wood grain, real steel corrugation, fabric texture and readable cargo geometry, moderate depth of field. No CGI look, no plastic skin, no dramatic advertising lighting, no extra workers, cranes or trucks, no captions, logos or watermarks.
