## Génération initiale

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: master reference photograph, scene 01 of the LabCongo cinematic website; fictional illustrative scene, not a record of actual project activity.
Primary request: exactly three adult representatives, two men and one woman, arriving together on foot toward the entrance of a credible European scientific research centre. This single image must establish recognizable character identities and the exterior location for later continuity.
Scene/backdrop: a modest, established European research campus, pale stone and glass facade, ordinary aluminium-framed entrance doors, practical windows and discreet mature landscaping. A real, maintained working institution with subtle signs of daily use. No futuristic architecture.
Subjects: three Black adults with distinct, natural faces, realistic body proportions and relaxed professional bearing. Character A, a man about 45, closely cropped hair with a little grey at the temples, short neat beard, navy blazer, white open-collar shirt, charcoal trousers, black leather shoes. Character B, a man about 35, short textured hair, clean-shaven face, charcoal overshirt over a pale blue shirt, beige chinos, brown leather shoes. Character C, a woman about 38, dark hair tied in a low bun, stone-coloured blazer, ivory blouse, navy trousers, black flat shoes. All are fully clothed, walking naturally, quietly talking with each other, not posing for an advertisement. Faces visible in natural three-quarter view and sufficiently clear to serve as a visual reference. Plausible hands and walking poses.
Style/medium: cinematic documentary photography with lifelike skin texture, subtle imperfections, cloth weave and natural creases, restrained colour grading, realistic optics. Camera at human eye height, approximately 35mm lens, moderate depth of field so people and building remain readable.
Composition/framing: one single wide landscape image, 16:9 aspect ratio. Show the three people from head to toe with generous breathing room around them. They are approaching the clearly visible entrance at a slight diagonal, camera positioned to see their faces, not only their backs. Compose the trio together near the middle of the frame, with room for a subtle camera push and responsive crop. Keep the building entrance legible. No collage, no panels, no character sheet.
Lighting/mood: soft natural daylight under a lightly overcast European sky; believable contact shadows, sober, welcoming and purposeful.
Constraints: exactly two men and one woman, no extra foreground people. No captions, lettering, labels, logos or watermarks; the site's real brand assets will be overlaid separately. No plastic skin, no waxy faces, no cartoon, no miniature, no CGI look, no exaggerated bokeh, no glossy advertising lighting.

Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: corrected master reference photograph for LabCongo scene 01.
Input image: the provided photograph is the edit target and the exact character, wardrobe and architectural reference.
Change only the staging and framing to clearly show ARRIVAL at the entrance with more camera/cropping space. Retain the exact same three individuals, their recognizable faces, skin, hairstyles, apparent ages, body builds, clothing, shoes and the woman's black shoulder bag. Retain the pale stone, aluminium and glass research-centre architecture, the soft natural daylight and documentary photographic finish.
Reframe as a wider 16:9 landscape photograph, stepping the camera back so the full-height trio occupies about 60 percent of the image height, with generous pavement below them and facade above them.
Place the glass entrance AHEAD of their direction of travel toward the right side of the frame, not behind their backs. Show them walking diagonally toward that entrance. The camera stands to the side near the approach to the entrance and sees clear front-three-quarter or three-quarter-profile faces as they converse naturally. The building facade recedes diagonally across the frame so the spatial relationship makes sense: the group has not yet reached the door. Keep the trio near the middle and fairly close together for continuity and potential responsive crop.
No added people, no captions, no lettering, no logos. Keep realistic anatomy, skin texture, clothing creases, restrained contrast, moderate depth of field and believable contact shadows. Preserve the visual identities and photographic look of the reference. This is one photograph, not a collage.
