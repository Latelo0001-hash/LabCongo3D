## Prompt de génération

Use case: photorealistic-natural, precise object continuity.
Asset type: LabCongo scene 05, equipment close-up reference photograph, fictional illustration.
Input image 1 is the exact microscope and workbench reference. Input image 2 establishes the same laboratory environment.
Create a single wide 16:9 documentary photograph showing a closer, slightly elevated three-quarter view of the SAME microscope standing on the SAME pale-grey workbench. Preserve its exact recognisable form: ivory-white upright and angled binocular head, two black eyepieces pointing left, silver multi-objective revolving nosepiece, black rectangular mechanical stage and substage optics, concentric black focusing knobs on the visible right side, muted blue-grey broad base and black cord. A real functional instrument with light scuffs and normal use, not a new futuristic product or toy. Do not redesign the microscope or add impossible lenses.
Place the microscope near the centre-left with breathing room and the full base, head and eyepieces visible, approximately 65 percent of image height. Beside it on the right are the SAME clean empty glass flasks and the SAME compact digital balance from the reference; glassware is not fused, overlapping randomly or filled with decorative liquids. The power cord is unplugged and loosely coiled next to the base in preparation for checking and transport. Equipment is cared for and reusable.
No people in this shot. Behind the workbench, retain the same grey-framed left windows, autumn daylight and cream cupboards, naturally softer but recognizable; moderate depth of field rather than exaggerated blur. The scene conveys equipment selected for another educational use, without a fabricated label or claim.
Photoreal documentary optics, real glass refraction, subtle surface texture, natural light from the left, sober colour grading, believable scale. No studio sweep, no dramatic advertising light, no CGI finish, no logos, no captions, no labels, no watermark, no collage.
